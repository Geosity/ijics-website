Journal role

Journal objective

The International Journal of Intelligent Control and Systems (IJICS) is a peer-reviewed, open access international journal. It publishes articles describing recent fundamental contributions and innovative applications in the field of intelligent control and intelligent systems. Its objective is to disseminate important and leading-edge information in this new field in a timely fashion.

IJICS connects advances in intelligent control with implementations that can be examined, reused, and extended.
02

Research coverage

Scope topics

IJICS covers theoretical and practical aspects of intelligent technologies and knowledge-based systems. The scope includes, but is not limited to:

Control theory and intelligent methods

Intelligent control methodologies, adaptive control, learning-based control, optimization, estimation, and decision methods.

Systems and integration

System architecture, integration, coordination, modeling, simulation, multi-agent systems, and networked control systems.

Robotics and automation

Robotics, automation, intelligent machines, unmanned systems, sensing, actuation, and intelligent manufacturing.

Computing and development

Hardware and software development, embedded platforms, knowledge-based systems, and tools for intelligent systems.

03

Article contribution

What IJICS publishes

Fundamental contributions

Research that advances the understanding, methods, architecture, modeling, or control of intelligent systems.

Innovative applications

Practical work that demonstrates new uses of intelligent control, automation, hardware, software, or integrated systems.

Submissions should make their contribution clear through:
  • Originality and scholarly significance
  • Sound methodology and analysis
  • Relevance to intelligent control and systems
  • Clear presentation of theory, evidence, or application
