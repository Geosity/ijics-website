The journal at a glance

Intelligent control research, open to the world

IJICS publishes original research, reviews, and authoritative perspectives that advance intelligent control theory and its application to autonomous systems, robotics, manufacturing, and complex systems engineering. The journal connects rigorous methodological work with emerging technologies and international practice.

  • Open AccessFree and immediate access to published articles.
  • No Article Processing ChargeNo APC for authors—ever.
  • 75.36% International Editorial BoardA global perspective and diverse expertise.
  • Rigorous Peer ReviewHigh standards for every publication.
  • Efficient Publication ProcessA streamlined path to publication.
